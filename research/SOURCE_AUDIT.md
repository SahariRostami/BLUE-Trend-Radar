# Source audit and research decisions

Snapshot: **20 October 2026**. Supporting technical record; the required write-up remains two pages.

## How sources are interpreted

- Adopted EU/UNECE legal text supports regulatory requirements, **not** achieved TRL or market adoption.
- CORDIS periodic results support the work **reported as done**; objectives do not count as completed demonstrations.
- Company and vendor announcements support vendor **claims** about products, not independent operating-fleet audits.
- Industry TRL refers to the specific application shown, with provisional marks where evidence is incomplete. R&D starting maturity is a **planning assumption**, not a demonstrated second TRL.
- Proposed research novelty is a **testable gap**, not a declaration that nobody else has tried it.

## Nearest related research

## Funding precision

The Commission's 2026–2027 Horizon Europe Work Programme names HORIZON-CL5-2027-03-D5-03 as an **Innovation Action (IA)** with **EUR 24M topic budget**, **EUR 5–7M indicative EU contribution per consortium project** (lump sum; the per-area figures of about EUR 7M for Area A and EUR 5M for Area B are consistent with roughly four funded projects, though that number was not confirmed), and planned **15 Dec 2026 / 14 Apr 2027** dates. CORDIS Area B describes tracking components, measuring operational loads, API-based data sharing and passport/Digital Twin connections. This is a potential *consortium proposal*, not a grant to BLUE.
