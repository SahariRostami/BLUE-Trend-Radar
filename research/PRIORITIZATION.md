## Criteria and weights

| Criterion | Weight | What is scored |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic fit | 30% | How directly the trend uses capabilities BLUE publicly lists (Cluster 5 profile, services profile, running projects). |
| R&D opportunity | 25% | Size of the unsolved, researchable gap left for BLUE (rd_frontier), not the size of the market. |
| Funding timing | 20% | A concrete funding call, regulatory date or customer signal within about 24 months. |
| Feasibility | 15% | Whether BLUE could realistically access data, partners and test assets for a first project. |
| Evidence confidence | 10% | Quality of the evidence behind the industry TRL (provisional = lower). |

Scale: 1 (weak) to 5 (strong) per criterion; score = weighted sum, maximum 5. Scores are analyst judgements written down so they can be challenged. They were set per criterion before computing totals and were not adjusted afterwards.

## What the scores say

- **T06 Passports & circular data** ranks first in 99% of weight variations and first with equal weights: the choice of CircuTwin-HD (built on T06) does not depend on a particular weighting.
- **T01 MCS heavy-duty powertrains** is the strongest alternative project; it scores lower mainly on feasibility, because BLUE would depend on a truck OEM for hardware.
- **The scores challenge one ring.** T02 Battery health analytics is in *Engage now* but ranks 6th on its own. It stays in Engage now because CircuTwin-HD needs the same remaining-life methods and data: engaging T02 is part of the T06 project, not a separate bet. If CircuTwin-HD is not pursued, T02 should move to *Build capability*.
- **Rings and scores answer different questions.** The ring says *when* to act; the score says *how attractive* the trend is for BLUE. T04 Virtual homologation scores high but stays in *Build capability* because no funding window is open yet.

## Rationale per trend

**T06 Digital product passports and data-driven circularity** (score 4.30)

- Fit 4/5: BLUE's profile names reuse, remanufacturing and second-life decisions, LCA and design for disassembly.
- R&D gap 5/5: Component-level passports linked to operating loads are research activities in call D5-03.
- Timing 5/5: Battery passport Feb 2027; call D5-03 opens Dec 2026.
- Feasib. 3/5: Needs OEM/operator data access and a consortium; the components are well defined.
- Evidence 4/5: Legal text plus an industry data-exchange pilot.

**T01 MCS-ready high-voltage powertrains for heavy-duty EVs** (score 4.05)

- Fit 4/5: HV powertrain integration and thermal simulation are listed BLUE capabilities.
- R&D gap 4/5: Higher-voltage modular HDV powertrains are still open (subject of call D5-05).
- Timing 5/5: Dedicated 2027 Horizon topic D5-05, same call deadline as D5-03.
- Feasib. 3/5: Needs a truck OEM and HV hardware access; BLUE would be an engineering partner, not the owner.
- Evidence 4/5: Live interoperable demo and series offer, but company-reported.

**T04 Simulation-credible virtual validation and homologation** (score 3.90)

- Fit 5/5: Virtual validation and homologation are BLUE services; Naples V&V centre.
- R&D gap 4/5: Credibility methods for bus/coach ADAS and shuttles are little documented.
- Timing 3/5: Regulation (UN R171) opens the door, but uptake is gradual and no call is targeted.
- Feasib. 4/5: Mostly in-house simulation work plus a technical service partner.
- Evidence 2/5: Provisional TRL: no public type approval using virtual evidence found.

**T03 Vehicle-specific and fleet digital twins** (score 3.85)

- Fit 5/5: Digital twins are a headline BLUE capability.
- R&D gap 4/5: Fleet-level, cross-OEM twins remain unsolved.
- Timing 3/5: No dedicated near-term call found; twins appear inside other topics.
- Feasib. 3/5: Requires multi-OEM data agreements.
- Evidence 3/5: One validated subsystem twin (MINDED); other evidence is project plans.

**T07 AI surrogate models for CAE** (score 3.50)

- Fit 4/5: Simulation is central to BLUE; AI-assisted design is in its profile.
- R&D gap 4/5: Trusted surrogates with quantified error are unsolved.
- Timing 2/5: No targeted call found; timing is driven by clients and tools.
- Feasib. 4/5: BLUE owns past CFD/FEM results that could train surrogates.
- Evidence 3/5: Product launch is vendor-reported.

**T02 Battery state-of-health and remaining-life analytics** (score 3.30)

- Fit 3/5: Adjacent: builds on BLUE's predictive-maintenance work but battery analytics is a specialist field.
- R&D gap 3/5: Commercial services exist; the open gap is OEM-independent remaining life with uncertainty.
- Timing 4/5: Battery passport (Feb 2027) creates demand for state-of-health data.
- Feasib. 3/5: Raw BMS data access from OEMs is the main barrier.
- Evidence 4/5: Commercial offer since 2020 plus peer-reviewed fleet study.

**T08 Hydrogen fuel-cell / battery hybrid buses** (score 3.20)

- Fit 4/5: BLUE already leads B4IA on hydrogen-hybrid minibuses.
- R&D gap 2/5: Mature technology; open questions are economic, not engineering.
- Timing 2/5: No new targeted call; market split with battery-electric still unclear.
- Feasib. 4/5: Existing B4IA know-how and partners.
- Evidence 5/5: Buses in regular public service in two cities.

**T05 Level-4 automated urban shuttles** (score 3.10)

- Fit 3/5: Extends B4IA Level-3 work, but L4 software stacks come from specialist AV firms.
